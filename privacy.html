Privacy Policy

Diamond Karigar (Android app) · Last updated: 13 October 2026

Diamond Karigar is a simple diary app for diamond workers (karigar). You use it to note diamonds made, rates, advance payments (upad) and monthly totals. This page explains what happens to your information.

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your own phone.
  • The app has no login and no account.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data.

What the app stores

The app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your diamond entries, dates, types, rates and totals
  • Your upad (advance) records
  • Your name and workshop name, if you choose to type them
  • App settings such as language, theme, text size and reminder time

This information is not sent to the developer or to any server.

Internet use

The app opens its screens from the website vinayparmar9055.github.io, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. Because of this, GitHub may see ordinary technical details such as your IP address when the app loads or updates, as any website host does. The developer does not receive or store this information, and no part of your diary entries is sent.

PDF and sharing

When you create a PDF or print a summary, it is made on your phone. Where you save or send it (WhatsApp, Drive, email and so on) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies to it.

Ads, analytics and permissions

  • The app currently shows no ads.
  • It uses no analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not ask for your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

If ads or other services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Deleting your data

Your data lives only on your phone, so you control it. Deleting an entry removes it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's data in your phone settings, removes everything permanently. We cannot recover it, because we do not have a copy. Please keep your own backup, such as a PDF, of anything important.

Children

The app is made for working adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
